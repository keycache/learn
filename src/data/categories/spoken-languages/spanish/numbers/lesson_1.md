# Spanish Numbers: From 1 to 1,000,000 🇪🇸

> **¡Hola!** Welcome to your Spanish numbers adventure! By the end of this lesson, you'll be able to count all the way to **one million** using simple patterns. Let's go! 🚀

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- ✅ Count **1–10** perfectly
- ✅ Build **11–99** using a simple formula
- ✅ Say **hundreds** (100–900) and **thousands** (1,000–9,000)
- ✅ Spot the **tricky exceptions** (so they don't trick you!)
- ✅ Combine everything to reach **1,000,000**

---

## 🔢 Part 1: The Foundation — Numbers 1–10

These are the **building blocks**. Memorize them well — everything else builds on these!

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation Guide | Memory Trick |
|--------|---------|---------------------|--------------|
| 1 | **uno** | *OO-noh* | Sounds like "you know" → **uno** |
| 2 | **dos** | *dohss* | Two **dos** (sounds like "dose") |
| 3 | **tres** | *trehss* | **Tres** rhymes with "mess" |
| 4 | **cuatro** | *KWAH-troh* | **Qua**-tro → think **qua**rter (4 quarters = $1) |
| 5 | **cinco** | *SEEN-koh* | **Cin**co → **cin**ema (5 letters!) |
| 6 | **seis** | *seh-eess* | **Seis** → **s**ay **s**ix fast! |
| 7 | **siete** | *SYEH-teh* | **Sie**te → **se**ven **te**th (7th) |
| 8 | **ocho** | *OH-choh* | **O**cho → **O**ctopus has **8** legs |
| 9 | **nueve** | *NWEH-beh* | **Nue**ve → **n**ew **v**ideo (9 letters!) |
| 10 | **diez** | *DYEHSS* | **Diez** → **d**ie**z** (sounds like "dice") |

> **🗣️ Practice Tip:** Say each number **out loud 3 times** while tapping your fingers. Muscle memory helps!

```text
Pronunciation Key:
- ll / y  = "y" as in "yes" (in most of Spain: "ly" as in "million")
- j       = "h" as in "hello" (stronger, raspier)
- z / c(e/i) = "th" as in "think" (Spain) OR "s" as in "sun" (Latin America)
- v / b   = both sound like a soft "b" (lips barely touch)
- rr      = rolled "r" (purr like a cat!)
```

## 🔢 Part 2: The "Teens" — 11–15 (THE EXCEPTIONS!)

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** Numbers **11–15** are **irregular**. They don't follow the pattern. You just have to memorize them.

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Memory Trick |
|--------|---------|---------------|--------------|
| 11 | **once** | *OHN-seh* | **Once** upon a time... (11th hour) |
| 12 | **doce** | *DOH-seh* | **Doce** → **do**zen = **12**! |
| 13 | **trece** | *TREH-seh* | **Trece** sounds like **tres** (3) + **ce** |
| 14 | **catorce** | *kah-TOHR-seh* | **Catorce** → **cat** + **orce** (14 lives?) |
| 15 | **quince** | *KEEN-seh* | **Quince** → **quin**ceañera (15th birthday party!) |

```text
🧠 PATTERN ALERT: Notice they ALL end in **-ce** (sounds like "seh")!
   once, doce, trece, catorce, quince
```

## 🔢 Part 3: The Formula — 16–19 ("Ten + Six", "Ten + Seven"...)

> **✅ GOOD NEWS:** From **16 onward**, Spanish uses a **simple formula**:

```
DIECI + (number 6-9)
```

| Number | Formula | Spanish | Pronunciation |
|--------|---------|---------|---------------|
| 16 | dieci + seis | **dieciséis** | *dyeh-see-SEH-eess* |
| 17 | dieci + siete | **diecisiete** | *dyeh-see-SYEH-teh* |
| 18 | dieci + ocho | **dieciocho** | *dyeh-see-OH-choh* |
| 19 | dieci + nueve | **diecinueve** | *dyeh-see-NWEH-beh* |

> **📝 Spelling Note:** **16, 22, 23, 26** have **accents** (tilde) to show stress:
> - **dieciséis** (stress on *séis*)
> - **veintidós** (stress on *dós*)
> - **veintitrés** (stress on *trés*)
> - **veintiséis** (stress on *séis*)

## 🔢 Part 4: The Tens — 20, 30, 40... 90

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Pattern |
|--------|---------|---------------|---------|
| 20 | **veinte** | *BEHN-teh* | **Base** for 21–29 |
| 30 | **treinta** | *TREHN-tah* | **tre**s (3) + **inta** |
| 40 | **cuarenta** | *kwah-REHN-tah* | **cuatro** (4) + **enta** |
| 50 | **cincuenta** | *seen-KWEHN-tah* | **cinco** (5) + **uenta** ⚠️ **EXCEPTION!** |
| 60 | **sesenta** | *seh-SEHN-tah* | **seis** (6) + **enta** |
| 70 | **setenta** | *seh-TEHN-tah* | **siete** (7) + **enta** |
| 80 | **ochenta** | *oh-CHEHN-tah* | **ocho** (8) + **enta** |
| 90 | **noventa** | *noh-BEHN-tah* | **nueve** (9) + **enta** ⚠️ **EXCEPTION!** |

> **⚡ KEY PATTERNS:**
> - **30, 40, 60, 70, 80**: Take the unit (tres, cuatro, seis, siete, ocho) + **-enta**
> - **50 (cincuenta)**: NOT *cinuenta* — **extra "cu"**!
> - **90 (noventa)**: NOT *nueventa* — **"nueve" becomes "noven"**!
> - **20 (veinte)**: Unique — it's the base for 21–29

## 🔢 Part 5: 21–29 — The "Veinti-" Formula

```
VEINTI + (number 1-9)
```

> **One word!** No spaces, no "y" (and). Just smash them together.

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| 21 | **veintiuno** | *behn-tee-OO-noh* |
| 22 | **veintidós** | *behn-tee-DOHSS* ⚠️ **accent!** |
| 23 | **veintitrés** | *behn-tee-TREHSS* ⚠️ **accent!** |
| 24 | **veinticuatro** | *behn-tee-KWAH-troh* |
| 25 | **veinticinco** | *behn-tee-SEEN-koh* |
| 26 | **veintiséis** | *behn-tee-SEH-eess* ⚠️ **accent!** |
| 27 | **veintisiete** | *behn-tee-SYEH-teh* |
| 28 | **veintiocho** | *behn-tee-OH-choh* |
| 29 | **veintinueve** | *behn-tee-NWEH-beh* |

> **🎯 Memory Hook:** Think **"VEINTI" = "Twenty-"** (like "twenty-one" → "twentyone")

## 🔢 Part 6: 31–99 — The "Tens + Y + Units" Formula

> **✅ EASY PATTERN:** From **31 onward**, use **"y" (and)** between tens and units.

```
(TENS) + Y + (UNITS)
```

| Example | Breakdown | Spanish |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| 31 | treinta + y + uno | **treinta y uno** |
| 42 | cuarenta + y + dos | **cuarenta y dos** |
| 57 | cincuenta + y + siete | **cincuenta y siete** |
| 68 | sesenta + y + ocho | **sesenta y ocho** |
| 73 | setenta + y + tres | **setenta y tres** |
| 89 | ochenta + y + nueve | **ochenta y nueve** |
| 99 | noventa + y + nueve | **noventa y nueve** |

> **🗣️ Say it like:** "TREIN-ta ee OO-noh" (the **y** = "ee")

## 🔢 Part 7: 100 — The Gateway to Hundreds

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Note |
|--------|---------|---------------|------|
| 100 | **cien** | *syehn* | **Only** when exactly 100! |
| 101–199 | **ciento** | *SYEHN-toh* | Use **ciento** for 101+ |

> **⚠️ CRITICAL RULE:**
> - **100 exactly** → **cien**
> - **101, 102, 150, 199** → **ciento uno, ciento dos, ciento cincuenta, ciento noventa y nueve**

```text
Think: "Cien" = "A hundred" (exact)
      "Ciento" = "One hundred and..." (plus more)
```

---

## 🔢 Part 8: Hundreds 200–900 — The Formula

```
(UNIT) + CIENTOS
```

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Pattern |
|--------|---------|---------------|---------|
| 200 | **doscientos** | *dohs-SYEHN-tohs* | dos + cientos |
| 300 | **trescientos** | *trehs-SYEHN-tohs* | tres + cientos |
| 400 | **cuatrocientos** | *kwah-troh-SYEHN-tohs* | cuatro + cientos |
| 500 | **quinientos** | *kee-NYEHN-tohs* ⚠️ | **NOT *cincocientos*!** |
| 600 | **seiscientos** | *seh-ees-SYEHN-tohs* | seis + cientos |
| 700 | **setecientos** | *seh-teh-SYEHN-tohs* ⚠️ | **NOT *sietecientos*!** |
| 800 | **ochocientos** | *oh-choh-SYEHN-tohs* | ocho + cientos |
| 900 | **novecientos** | *noh-beh-SYEHN-tohs* ⚠️ | **NOT *nuevecientos*!** |

> **🚨 THREE BIG EXCEPTIONS (memorize these!):**
> - **500** → **quinientos** (not *cincocientos*)
> - **700** → **setecientos** (not *sietecientos*)
> - **900** → **novecientos** (not *nuevecientos*)

> **💡 Memory Tricks:**
> - **Quin**ientos → **Quin**ce (15) → **Quin** = 5-ish
> - **Sete**cientos → **Sete** = 7 (short for *siete*)
> - **Nove**cientos → **Nove** = 9 (short for *nueve*)

## 🔢 Part 9: Numbers 101–999 — Putting It Together

```
(HUNDREDS) + (TENS + Y + UNITS)
```

| Number | Breakdown | Spanish |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| 101 | ciento + uno | **ciento uno** |
| 115 | ciento + quince | **ciento quince** |
| 237 | doscientos + treinta y siete | **doscientos treinta y siete** |
| 456 | cuatrocientos + cincuenta y seis | **cuatrocientos cincuenta y seis** |
| 599 | quinientos + noventa y nueve | **quinientos noventa y nueve** |
| 742 | setecientos + cuarenta y dos | **setecientos cuarenta y dos** |
| 999 | novecientos + noventa y nueve | **novecientos noventa y nueve** |

> **✅ NO "Y" AFTER HUNDREDS!** Just say the hundreds, then the rest.
> ✅ **Ciento** (not *cien*) for 101–199
> ✅ Use the **exception hundreds** (quinientos, setecientos, novecientos)

## 🔢 Part 10: Thousands — 1,000 to 9,000

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Pattern |
|--------|---------|---------------|---------|
| 1,000 | **mil** | *meel* | **Just "mil"** — no "un"! |
| 2,000 | **dos mil** | *dohs meel* | dos + mil |
| 3,000 | **tres mil** | *trehs meel* | tres + mil |
| 4,000 | **cuatro mil** | *KWAH-troh meel* | cuatro + mil |
| 5,000 | **cinco mil** | *SEEN-koh meel* | cinco + mil |
| 6,000 | **seis mil** | *seh-eess meel* | seis + mil |
| 7,000 | **siete mil** | *SYEH-teh meel* | siete + mil |
| 8,000 | **ocho mil** | *OH-choh meel* | ocho + mil |
| 9,000 | **nueve mil** | *NWEH-beh meel* | nueve + mil |

> **🎯 GOLDEN RULE:** **Never say "un mil"** — it's just **"mil"**!
> (Unlike English "one thousand")

---

## 🔢 Part 11: Big Numbers — 1,001 to 999,999

```
(THOUSANDS) + (HUNDREDS) + (TENS + Y + UNITS)
```

| Number | Breakdown | Spanish |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| 1,001 | mil + uno | **mil uno** |
| 1,100 | mil + cien | **mil cien** |
| 1,234 | mil + doscientos + treinta y cuatro | **mil doscientos treinta y cuatro** |
| 2,000 | dos mil | **dos mil** |
| 15,000 | quince mil | **quince mil** |
| 100,000 | cien mil | **cien mil** |
| 200,000 | doscientos mil | **doscientos mil** |
| 999,999 | novecientos noventa y nueve mil + novecientos noventa y nueve | **novecientos noventa y nueve mil novecientos noventa y nueve** |

> **📌 NO "Y" BETWEEN THOUSANDS AND HUNDREDS!**
> - ✅ **mil doscientos** (not *mil y doscientos*)
> - ✅ **dos mil trescientos**

---

## 🔢 Part 12: ONE MILLION! 🎉

| Number | Spanish | Pronunciation | Note |
|--------|---------|---------------|------|
| 1,000,000 | **un millón** | *oon mee-YOHN* | **Must say "un"** here! |
| 2,000,000 | **dos millones** | *dohs mee-YOH-nehss* | **Millones** (plural!) |
| 1,000,001 | **un millón uno** | *oon mee-YOHN OO-noh* | No "y"! |

> **⚠️ KEY DIFFERENCE FROM "MIL":**
> - **mil** → never "un mil"
> - **millón** → **MUST say "un millón"**
> - **Millones** adds **-es** in plural (not just -s)

```text
🧠 MEMORY: "Millón" sounds like "million" — but don't forget the ACCENT on the ó!
```
